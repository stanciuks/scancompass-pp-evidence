SESSION RESOLUTION
Typed outcomes:
- VALID {accountId, sessionId}
- ABSENT
- REJECTED: MALFORMED / UNKNOWN / EXPIRED / IDLE / ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE

Deterministic order:
ABSENT -> MALFORMED -> UNKNOWN -> EXPIRED -> IDLE -> ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE -> VALID

Malformed tokens are rejected before PostgreSQL.
Rejected sessions are not refreshed.
Unexpected database errors propagate rather than being converted to anonymous/unknown.
