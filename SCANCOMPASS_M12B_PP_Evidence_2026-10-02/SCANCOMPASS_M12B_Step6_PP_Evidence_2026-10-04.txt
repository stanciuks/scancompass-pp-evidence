2. PURPOSE OF THE STEP

M12B-6 validates the implemented account, session, signup and recovery subsystem
against disposable PostgreSQL, without deploying it. Its central restore test
covers three ephemeral bearer-state classes: sessions, existing-account email
challenges, and pending-signup challenges. Restoring an older database snapshot
can revive previously consumed or revoked rows; the acceptance therefore needs
both a positive resurrection control and an independent sanitized restore. [S4]

The recorded acceptance includes real account/session storage, durable lockout,
a genuinely separate restart process, route-level feature-gate tests, a documented
tombstone transaction under the application role, least-privilege probes, exact
constraint membership, and a real logical pg_dump/pg_restore rehearsal. It does
not constitute live HTTP, production email, or production backup acceptance. [S4]

3. THE FINAL BLOCKING FINDING AND CORRECTION

Before this correction, runRestartChild() installed child close/error handlers
that could settle its outer promise while handOffDetachedChild() was still
awaiting process-group termination. The caller then removed the handoff and
acknowledgement files. A later refusal or UNKNOWN settlement could be discarded
by the caller's already-settled flag. The helper and focused test wrapper awaited
the right result, but the actual integration caller did not. [S1,S2,S3,S4]

The correction introduces one shared coordinator, superviseDetachedRun(), and one
result arbiter, arbitrateDetachedRun(). Both the integration suite and focused
regressions use that implementation. Child events are recorded separately from
the ownership decision; the handoff is awaited before the final outcome is formed.
Refused ownership remains a failure even when the child exits with code zero.
UNKNOWN termination remains explicitly unresolved. File release is performed in
the shared wrapper after the required awaits. [S1,S2,S3]

The distinction matters because the group leader can close while another process
in the same group remains alive. The corrected refusal path waits for the group
settlement result rather than treating the first child event as sufficient. [S2,S3]
