11. RESULT, AND THE GAP FOUND IMMEDIATELY AFTER

M12B-7 is CLOSED. Production now has migration 0002 applied, the M12B
account/session schema live, the M12B candidate application live, anonymous
scanning enabled and re-proven, and public signup, account email and report
email all closed.

A source audit for the next step then found that the controlled operator
account required by M12B-7A CANNOT be created while public signup remains
closed. At the deployed commit:
  - the only signup-challenge insertion path is reached through
    POST /api/account/signup;
  - that route returns before challenge creation while
    ACCOUNT_SIGNUP_ENABLED=0;
  - password-reset challenge creation requires an existing account;
  - there are no accounts;
  - /api/account/verify can complete a valid signup challenge but cannot
    mint one;
  - there is no committed operator-account or invite CLI.

The result is a closed loop:
    no account       -> reset cannot mint a challenge
    signup closed    -> signup cannot mint a challenge
    verify           -> needs a challenge that cannot exist

This was treated as a SOURCE / PRODUCT GAP rather than bypassed with
hand-written SQL. The recommended smallest fix was a reviewed one-shot
operator invite CLI reusing the existing signup-challenge service and delivery
path while bypassing only the public signup HTTP gate — no schema change, no
public route expansion, and no direct credential or challenge-row fabrication.
That fix is recorded separately as M12B-7A. [S3,S4]
