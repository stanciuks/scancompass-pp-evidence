DURABLE LOCKOUT
Constants:
- threshold: 10 failures
- observation window: 15 minutes
- lockout duration: 15 minutes

Behavior:
- first failure starts a window
- failures inside the window increment
- threshold sets locked_until
- failures while locked do not extend the lock
- lock expires without operator action
- first failure after expiry starts a clean window
- successful authentication/reset can clear all three lockout fields
