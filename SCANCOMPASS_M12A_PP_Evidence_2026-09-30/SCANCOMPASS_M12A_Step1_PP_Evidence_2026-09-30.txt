PURPOSE
Define the only command/result vocabulary allowed across the future web↔scanner-worker boundary.

IMPLEMENTED
- Protocol version fixed at v: 1.
- Strict worker command schema with executionId, canonical hostname, remaining budget,
  legacy-TLS/RDAP capability booleans, and three configured identity values.
- Strict result envelopes: COMPLETED, ABORTED and REFUSED.
- REFUSED codes: IDENTITY_REJECTED, COMMAND_INVALID, BUSY.
- FreeScanResult is strictly validated at the boundary.
- Unknown fields are rejected at every relevant object level.
- No coercion, passthrough, schema defaults or silent repair.
- Compile-time drift guards were added so protocol schema and scanner model cannot silently diverge.

IMPORTANT CORRECTIVE REVIEW
Two initially invented limits were removed:
- MAX_RESULT_BYTES = 1 MiB had no authoritative source.
- MAX_IDENTITY_FIELD_LENGTH = 256 was proven wrong because current valid publicBaseUrl values
  can exceed it.

The replacement principle was:
Do not introduce protocol-only policy unless it is derived from an existing authority or proven
not to narrow the authoritative validation rules.

APPROVED BUDGET BEHAVIOUR
budgetMs must be an integer in 1..SCAN_BUDGET_MS.
Out-of-range values are rejected; they are not silently clamped by the schema.
