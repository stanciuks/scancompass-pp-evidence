2. Identity
   - Invalid/placeholder identity is a hard blocker in production.
   - Zero target traffic is allowed on identity refusal.
   - The development placeholder remains a development affordance only.
   - No placeholder identity may reach a third party in production.

3. Restart semantics
   - restart: unless-stopped was accepted.
   - Restart restores worker availability only.
   - There is no queue, job persistence, resumption or automatic re-submission of work.

4. Egress contract
   - Host policy must be derived from the same address-policy authority rather than a hand-written
     list of a few familiar private/special ranges.
   - IPv6 must follow the policy's allowlist framing.
   - DNS behaviour must be measured before host firewall rules are written.
   - E1–E23 acceptance cases were defined for later proof.

FINAL OWNER DECISIONS
- Current host: use it; measure before considering resize.
- Worker hostname logging: NO.
- Restart policy: unless-stopped; never retry an in-flight scan automatically.
- Invalid identity: refuse scan; no fallback.
- Replay: no protocol bearer secret; residual risk documented.
- Initial resource envelope: conservative starting ceiling, not a measured worker requirement.
- Egress: derive from the authoritative address-policy source; do not hand-maintain two definitions.
