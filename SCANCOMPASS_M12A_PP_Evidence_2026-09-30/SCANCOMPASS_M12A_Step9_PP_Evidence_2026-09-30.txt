FINAL PERFORMANCE INTERPRETATION
The literal raw pooled percentile inequality was not numerically satisfied, and the evidence keeps
that fact. Direct decomposition showed only ~4–6 ms attributable to the process/protocol boundary,
while larger raw end-to-end differences varied with remote-host timing. The closure therefore used
the authoritative requirement — no persistent performance regression attributable to the extraction
— rather than forcing a noisy Internet benchmark to resolve a few-millisecond effect.

CANCELLATION TEST RACE
An intermittent M12A-5 test race was found: bytes were snapshotted before controller.abort(),
while multiple target requests were still concurrently propagating cancellation.

Production cancellation code was unchanged.

The test now checks the actual property: once the worker deadline signal is already aborted,
no further target write occurs. Existing assertions for CALLER_GONE, CALLER_ABORTED, socket
destruction, no new connection, permit release, no retry/resumption and clean completion remain.

Focused stability: 10/10 consecutive runs passed, with repeated full gates also passing.
