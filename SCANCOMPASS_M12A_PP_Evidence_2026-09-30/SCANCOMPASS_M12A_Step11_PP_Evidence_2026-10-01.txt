IMPORTANT M12A-12 BLOCKER FOUND
Boot timing showed:
- Nginx accepting: 15:24:21.274 UTC
- app/scanner containers started: 15:24:23.707
- Docker service started: 15:24:24.909
- firewall policy active: 15:24:25.104
- app application process starting: 15:24:25.644

On this boot the firewall became active 540 ms before the app could serve, but this order is not
architecturally guaranteed.

Nginx was already accepting approximately 3.83 seconds before the host egress policy existed, and
the firewall apply script may wait up to 90 seconds for the scanner bridge in some boot scenarios.

Therefore scanning MUST NOT be reopened until M12A-12 introduces and proves an explicit startup
ordering/interlock that guarantees public scan execution cannot become available before the host
egress policy.

This blocker was recorded rather than patched ad hoc during deployment.
