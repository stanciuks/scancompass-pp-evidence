Problem discovered in M12A-11
A controlled production reboot showed:
- nginx public edge accepting at 15:24:21.274 UTC
- scanner-egress policy active at 15:24:25.104
- application able to serve at 15:24:25.644

The firewall happened to become active 540 ms before the app was ready, but this was not an
architectural guarantee. The public edge was accepting approximately 3.83 seconds before the host
egress policy existed, and the firewall apply path can wait up to 90 seconds for the Docker bridge.
Therefore scanning could not safely be reopened until startup ordering was explicitly enforced.

Selected interlock
A systemd drop-in for nginx:

[Unit]
Requires=scancompass-scanner-egress.service
After=scancompass-scanner-egress.service

Repository artifact:
deploy/systemd/nginx.service.d/scancompass-egress.conf

Intended production install path:
/etc/systemd/system/nginx.service.d/scancompass-egress.conf

Why this design
- After= guarantees nginx is ordered after the egress-policy unit.
- Requires= makes a failed policy activation prevent nginx from starting.
- A systemd drop-in survives vendor nginx package upgrades.
- No PartOf= or ExecStop= was added, avoiding the lifecycle failure discovered during M12A-10.
- The application does not need to parse nftables state or gain another launch control.

Disposable Linux proof
The dependency semantics were tested under systemd 255:
- success: policy finished before nginx activated
- failure: policy failed and nginx never activated
- boot success: Docker -> policy -> nginx
- boot failure: policy failed and nginx remained inactive
- stopping policy stopped nginx
- restarting policy restarted nginx after the policy reapplied
- recovery after clearing the fault succeeded
- systemd-analyze verify reported no dependency cycle

Repository structural proof
14 focused tests pin:
- exact Requires= dependency
- exact After= dependency
- no Wants= substitution
- no PartOf=, ExecStop=, BindsTo= or Restart=
- no reverse nginx reference in the egress unit
- Docker ordering retained
- committed ordering graph acyclic
- drop-in contains only [Unit] dependency directives, not nginx routing/TLS configuration
