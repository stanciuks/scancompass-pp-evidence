1. WHAT M14 IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

M14 lets an authenticated account that has already proved it controls an exact
hostname spend one purchased entitlement on one deeper assessment of that
hostname, and keep the resulting report for ninety days. The preceding milestone
built the commercial half — orders, provider events and entitlements — and
deliberately stopped before anything could be consumed. M14 is the delivery
half. Its central safety property is that THE ASSESSMENT TIER IS NEVER AN INPUT:
it cannot be supplied in a body, query, header, cookie or debug flag, and is
only ever the fact that execution reached a module reachable solely from an
orchestrator holding a committed reservation. Its second property is that AN
ENTITLEMENT IS NOT SCANNING AUTHORITY — using one requires the account, the
exact hostname and a CURRENT domain authorisation, re-checked at reservation and
again at consumption. [S2]
