1. WHAT M14 IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

M14 lets an authenticated account that has already proved it controls an exact
hostname spend one purchased entitlement on one deeper assessment of that
hostname, and keep the resulting report for ninety days. The preceding milestone
built the commercial half — orders, provider events and entitlements — and
deliberately stopped before anything could be consumed. M14 is the delivery
half. Its central safety property is that THE ASSESSMENT TIER IS NEVER AN INPUT:
it cannot be supplied in a body, query, header, cookie or debug flag, and is
only ever the fact that execution reached a module reachable solely from an
orchestrator holding a committed reservation. Its second property is that AN
ENTITLEMENT IS NOT SCANNING AUTHORITY — using one requires the account, the
exact hostname and a CURRENT domain authorisation, re-checked at reservation and
again at consumption. [S2]

4. WHAT IS PROVEN ACROSS THIS PACKAGE, AND WHAT IS NOT

PROVEN, on a disposable PostgreSQL 18.6 and reported by the implementing
sessions: that the migration is additive and widening and applies cleanly; that
its measured catalogue equals the figures the design predicted BEFORE anything
was measured; that no runtime write path of the preceding milestone fails
against it, and that the older rollback image's write path, readiness and
retention pass too, with a control; that the referential behaviour holds under
the application role with a privilege revoked; that a real dump and restore
round trip survives it; that the designed purge pass DEADLOCKS and the shipped
non-waiting form does not, with the deadlock kept as a negative control; and
that consumption cannot commit without the whole durable report, proven by
seven injected faults that each rolled back.

NOT PROVEN, AND NOT CLAIMED ANYWHERE IN THIS PACKAGE: the migration's timing
against a restored production-shaped copy — no safe off-production copy was
available, and production was NOT contacted to obtain one; an explicit
real-database run of the guarded suites as a release gate, which ordinary
continuous integration SKIPS; the authorisation-callback guard, which cannot be
pinned until a caller exists; probe-list MEMBERSHIP, as distinct from identifier
shape; and every claim about the ten extended checks, which DO NOT EXIST IN
CODE.
