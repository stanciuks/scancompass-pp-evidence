3. THE THREE OWNER APPROVALS, AS GIVEN (OWNER-DECIDED) [S2]

The approvals were given on 11 October 2026 as the instruction for this
documentation change. The committed record carries the owner's words; the
substance of each, as recorded, is below. The committed source is the
authoritative wording.

  H4 — HOST SCOPE. An authorisation covers ONE EXACT HOSTNAME. Extended
  assessments initiate requests only at that hostname, on the fixed path list,
  and follow redirects only under the EXISTING FREE transport policy: same
  registrable site, no HTTPS downgrade, at most five redirects, and every
  existing address and safety check. The extended profile NEVER DELIBERATELY
  SELECTS ANOTHER HOSTNAME. Facts taken from a redirect destination must be
  attributed to the host that served them, and an unsupported cross-host
  comparison returns UNKNOWN.

  The approval states its own limit: it PRESERVES CURRENT FREE BEHAVIOUR and
  APPROVES NO additional scanning, no new ports, no new paths, no arbitrary
  subdomains and no broader authorisation.

  M3 — CONTROL-COUNT PRESENTATION. Option b-prime. Every existing
  ControlCounts bucket, its numerical semantics and the strict public-report-v1
  schema are PRESERVED UNCHANGED. For EXTENDED reports only, a truthful
  supplementary explanation identifies the extended checks that are assessed
  but not scored in this version, with its count DERIVED FROM THE ACTUAL
  EXTENDED FINDINGS and not from a presumed FREE baseline. Genuinely
  inapplicable FREE controls are NOT relabelled. FREE presentation is
  unchanged. The wording belongs to OD-10.

  M4 — EXTENDED PRIORITY. The EXISTING SEVERITY-FIRST comparator is retained
  unchanged: severity, then foundational status, then recoverable points, then
  confidence, then effort, then a stable identifier. The new
  priority-extended-v1 methodology may admit eligible ZERO-POINT extended
  corrective findings, and each such action MUST CLEARLY STATE that correcting
  it does not increase the security score in this version. FREE keeps
  priority-v1 and its existing output unchanged.

WHAT THE THREE HAVE IN COMMON is that each chooses the conservative direction
and each writes its own boundary into the approval. H4 says what it does not
widen. M3 says what it does not relabel and whose wording it is not. M4 says
what it does not change and what the user must be told.
