3. WHAT CHANGED, AND THE TWO THINGS DELIBERATELY LEFT GLOBAL
   (SOURCE-RECORDED) [S2]

Every complete-catalogue iteration under src/ was audited. The changes, by
purpose:

  THE CATALOGUE gains a tier on every entry, selection and membership helpers
  for a tier, a per-tier catalogue version, a refusal error type, and tier
  arguments on the identifier and scorable-check helpers, all DEFAULTING TO
  FREE. TIERS NEST BY DESIGN: a FREE check belongs to both tiers, an EXTENDED
  check only to EXTENDED.
  SCORING now synthesises missing results FOR THE SCORED TIER ONLY — this is a
  named design hazard, because synthesising a paid check's absence into a free
  score would move the free score. An OUT-OF-TIER RESULT is treated exactly
  like an unknown identifier: excluded, with an integrity problem recorded.
  The stamped catalogue version is chosen by tier.
  REPORT COMPOSITION composes findings only for the tier's checks, replacing a
  whole-catalogue iteration that would otherwise have thrown.
  THE ENGINE takes its not-produced list, its undecidable results and its
  stamped versions FROM THE FREE TIER explicitly.
  THE WORKER RESULT CEILING is derived from the FREE tier's count and is
  UNCHANGED AT 546,816 BYTES. A per-tier selector was added and IS READ BY
  NOTHING until M14-3's protocol work.
  VERSION SELECTION replaces six imported constants with per-tier selectors.
  Each returns EVERY EXISTING FREE LITERAL UNCHANGED and THROWS for EXTENDED.

8. P2-4: THE GUARD THAT WAS ADDED, AND THE CONTRACT THAT WAS DEFERRED
   [S1,S2]

THE FINDING. The free-scan result carries NO TIER, and both scoring and report
composition DEFAULT TO FREE. So a future extended scan could, in principle, be
composed and scored UNDER FREE RULES — a paid assessment silently graded as a
free one.

WHY THE FULL FIX WAS NOT AVAILABLE IN THIS SLICE, AS RECORDED. The production
call path passes scoring only the hostname, the mail-assessment domain and the
results, SO A GUARD INSIDE SCORING COULD NOT SEE THE PROFILE AT ALL. Report
composition, by contrast, receives the WHOLE SCAN, immediately after scoring
and inside the same error-handling block. That is the one place a guard could
stand today.

WHAT WAS ADDED — NARROW, AND FAIL-CLOSED:

  - the extended profile version literal is RESERVED in source and RECOGNISED
    ONLY IN ORDER TO REFUSE IT. NOTHING STAMPS IT, and the version selector
    for EXTENDED still throws.
  - a composability assertion runs FIRST in report composition, reading the
    profile FROM THE SCAN ITSELF rather than from an argument a caller
    controls. An extended-stamped scan is REFUSED for any tier but EXTENDED,
    AND EXTENDED IS ITSELF STILL REFUSED because it has no versions. The
    practical effect today is that an extended-stamped scan CANNOT PRODUCE A
    REPORT AT ALL.

HOW THE REFUSAL WAS PROVEN, at three levels, by eight tests: report
composition with the default tier and with an explicit FREE tier; the explicit
EXTENDED tier, still refused; and END TO END through the assessment runner,
where a worker returning an extended-stamped scan yields a FAILED run with an
internal-failure reason AND NO REPORT. The same tests confirm that a
FREE-stamped scan still composes and that the existing test fixture profile is
still tolerated. REMOVING THE GUARD MAKES THREE OF THE EIGHT TESTS FAIL —
which is what makes them a guard rather than decoration. [S2]

WHY FREE STILL CANNOT MOVE: every FREE scan stamps the free profile, NO TIER
FIELD WAS ADDED to the scan result, so its serialised shape is unchanged, and
the golden is unchanged. [S2]

WHAT IS EXPLICITLY NOT DONE, AND IS MANDATORY AT STEP 2.3. The full contract is
written into the design as a stop and a new section: the scoring and
composition tier must be DERIVED FROM THE SCAN'S SERVER-SELECTED PROFILE, so a
caller can neither supply it independently nor rely on a default; profile and
tier must AGREE IN BOTH DIRECTIONS; an UNKNOWN PROFILE MUST BE REFUSED, with
fixtures moved to a real profile identifier or an explicit seam; scoring must
be BOUND TO THE PROFILE; and STEP 2.3 IS NOT ACCEPTABLE WITHOUT A NEGATIVE
TEST, both direct and through the runner.

THE RECORD IS EXPLICIT ABOUT WHAT THIS IS: an ENGINEERING-STAGE BOUNDARY, NOT A
RELAXATION OF SCAN VERIFICATION, and it makes no EXTENDED admission reachable.
This note repeats that framing because the distinction is the only thing
separating a deferred requirement from a quietly accepted hole. [S1,S2]
