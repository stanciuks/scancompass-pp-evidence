2. WHAT M14-2.4A IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS. Four of the ten paid checks, written as PURE FUNCTIONS that take
already-collected observations and return a result. They were chosen because
each depends on NONE of the five open owner semantic decisions, so each could be
built without pre-empting a decision the owner has not taken. [S2]

THE FOUR, BY PURPOSE AND NOT BY IDENTIFIER. [S2]

  1. INSECURE SUBMISSION TARGETS. Whether the secure page contains a form whose
     submission would travel without encryption. The only one of the four that
     can report a non-informational defect.
  2. TRANSPORT AND FRAMING HEADER CONSISTENCY. Whether the response repeats a
     policy header with values that would instruct a browser differently. The
     content-security policy is RECORDED BUT NEVER JUDGED, by design and by
     explicit stop condition.
  3. PRELOAD PREREQUISITES. Whether the four locally observable preconditions
     for the strongest form of transport security are met. Informational. IT
     MAY NEVER CLAIM ANYTHING ABOUT LIST ELIGIBILITY, SUBMISSION, ACCEPTANCE OR
     MEMBERSHIP, and that prohibition is asserted on every path.
  4. SIGNED-DELEGATION PUBLICATION. Whether the registry publishes the facts
     that indicate a signed delegation. Informational, derived ONLY from the
     registration facts the earlier steps already extracted, WITH NO NEW LOOKUP
     AND NO VALIDATING RESOLVER, and every result that read those facts carries
     an explicit marker that validation WAS NOT ASSESSED.

WHAT THEY RECEIVE, AND WHAT THEY CANNOT RECEIVE. Only already-collected facts:
the parsed-document facts from step 2.2, a read-only header accessor, a closed
token describing the plain-text root's first redirect, or the registration
facts — plus an injected observation time. NO PARAMETER IS A RESOLVER, CLIENT,
FETCH CALLBACK, URL FETCHER OR PAGE BODY, and no check reads a clock, the
environment or any caller-supplied tier selection. EACH OF THOSE IS ASSERTED BY
A BOUNDARY TEST. [S2]

EVERY CHECK IS TOTAL, WHICH IS A STRONGER PROPERTY THAN IT SOUNDS. A hostile
fact object — including one whose property access throws — is CONTAINED as an
undetermined result, never as a thrown error and NEVER AS A REASSURING ANSWER.
That last clause is the one the review tested hardest, and the one it found a
hole in. [S2]

WHAT IT IS NOT. Not wired into anything. No catalogue, scoring, enrichment,
content or evidence-policy entry; no report composition; no engine integration;
no worker protocol field; no route; no admission rule. Not the remaining six
checks. NOT ANY OF THE FIVE OPEN OWNER SEMANTIC DECISIONS. Not a fix for any
free-path finding. Not a schema or migration change. [S2]

WHAT THE OUTPUTS MAY CONTAIN IS BOUNDED IN ADVANCE. Only pre-existing status,
reason and severity tokens — no new vocabulary. At most eight flat evidence
items, each at most two hundred characters. The customer-facing text parameters
are ALWAYS EMPTY, because customer copy belongs to step 2.5 and has not been
approved. NEVER A PATH, QUERY, FRAGMENT, FIELD NAME, FIELD VALUE, HEADER VALUE
OR CRYPTOGRAPHIC DIGEST. Attempts to place each of those into evidence are
tested and refused. [S2]

SOURCE. Where password-field ownership was neither confirmed nor positively
excluded, the slice answered the HIGHEST severity with lowered confidence. The
memo says the highest severity applies when a password field is INSIDE such a

  The correction answers the middle severity with an explicit
  unresolved-ownership flag, and distinguishes THREE states rather than two:
  ownership confirmed; ownership positively established as absent; and
  ownership not established either way. Confidence stays at its highest,
  because THE INSECURE TARGET ITSELF IS CONFIRMED — only the password question
  is open. An unrelated, unowned password field still never escalates. [S2]

  AND THE CORRECTING SESSION TRACED WHY THE BRANCH IS UNREACHABLE WITH REAL
  FACTS, WHICH IS WHY THE DOWNGRADE COSTS NOTHING. The unresolved count is
  incremented only when the parser's own form pointer names a form the tree
  walk never reached; such a form is NOT AMONG THE WALKED FORMS and therefore
  cannot be one of the reported insecure ones. So an unresolved field is an
  UNEXAMINED OWNER, never a hidden owned password of a reported form. [S2]

AREA TWO — THE NINE SURVIVING MUTANTS. Covered in section 5. Eight tested, one
recorded as an assumption. [S2]

AREA THREE — THE DUPLICATE-HEADER SEMANTICS. BEHAVIOUR DELIBERATELY LEFT

either branch, on the explicit ground that CHANGING EITHER WOULD ADOPT AN

labelled tests and wrote up the options. [S2]

  THE FACTS IT CHECKED, BY READING THE SPECIFICATIONS AND NOT BY TESTING A
  BROWSER, and it says so. The transport-security header has no list syntax; a
  browser receiving more than one occurrence processes ONLY THE FIRST; a
